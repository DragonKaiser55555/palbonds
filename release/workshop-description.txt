Ever wanted to just... pet a wild Pal?

Not knock it out first. Not throw a sphere at it. Just walk up to the little
guy, scratch its head, give it a berry, and see what happens.

That's the whole mod.


[h2]How it goes[/h2]

You find a Pal. You pet it. It's suspicious. You feed it something. It's less
suspicious. You keep at it, and at some point it decides you're alright and
starts trailing after you.

Then it follows you around. It fights for you when something jumps you. And if
you keep it happy long enough, it joins your party on its own - no sphere, no
fight, it just decides it's going with you.

Yes, you can have a whole entourage. No, that is not always a good idea. Six
Pals swinging at the same Chikipi is exactly as chaotic as it sounds, and if
they clip each other in the process, that's between them and you.


[h2]Every wild Pal has a personality now[/h2]

There's a tag above their head telling you who you're dealing with before you
walk over:

[list]
[*][b]Normal[/b] - whatever its species normally does
[*][b]Curious[/b] - stops and looks at you as you come closer
[*][b]Timid[/b] - nope, gone
[*][b]Aloof[/b] - you do not exist
[*][b]Grumpy[/b] - all posturing, until you look like easy prey
[*][b]Hostile[/b] - straight for you
[*][b]Feral[/b] - straight for anything
[/list]

Some Pals are simply not going to be your friend today, and that's fine. Find
another one.

Tags bother you? F9 turns them off.


[h2]They remember what you do[/h2]

Hit a Pal that trusts you and it flinches. Do it again and that's it - it's
done with you. It won't follow you, won't let you feed it, won't come back. And
it might decide it has something to say about it.

Wander too far and leave one behind, and it gives up on you too. Less dramatic,
but you still lost it.


[h2]Controls[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]4[/b] - the usual radial menu. Pet, feed.
[*][b]F8[/b] - play with it
[*][b]F9[/b] - hide/show the personality tags
[/list]

Get close, look right at it. About five metres.


[h2]Requirements[/h2]

UE4SS - it's listed as required, so Steam grabs it for you.


[h2]A few things[/h2]

[list]
[*]Bonding takes a while. That's the point. Interactions do most of the work,
but a Pal walking with you slowly warms up to you anyway.
[*]Don't leave a bonded Pal behind. Distance costs you trust.
[*]High-level Pals take longer. Low-level ones come around fast.
[*]A Pal that joins you this way shows up already fond of you. You earned it.
[/list]
